Outlook Mode

Outlook-style features for Thunderbird. Changes apply to new compose windows and newly opened folders.
Sync Reading pane Signature Cleanup Reply & pins Sending Scheduled mail @Mentions Quick Steps Shortcuts Diagnostics

Sync between devices

Turn it on first on the device whose settings you want to keep; a device that joins later takes the synced values (Undo puts its own back). Each account must be added once on every device: passwords, sign-ins, encryption keys and file paths are never synced. The data is kept in a hidden folder “Outlook Mode Sync” in your mailbox; Thunderbird syncs every 10 minutes and at startup. Pins sync on their own (see above).

      

        
        
      

      
      
Address suggestions then know everyone you have been in touch with. With “Local address books” synced above, these contacts are the same on all your devices. Addresses already in an address book, your own addresses and no-reply senders are skipped.

      

      

        
      

      
      
In Address Book → New Contact: paste Name (Contoso-RND-HN) <address> (as copied from a To/From line) into the email field, or type the address: its name is taken from your address books and mail. Picking a code sets Company, Department and the (…) after the display name. Only data on this computer is used.

      
      
    


    
Reading pane

    

      
      
      
      
      
      
      
      
      
      
      
      
Works in the conversation view of the Thunderbird Conversations add-on. Reply buttons, Ctrl+R, Ctrl+Shift+R, Ctrl+L and Thunderbird's own Reply/Forward buttons open the reply above the newest message; the original is quoted below your text when it is sent. Undo Send applies. Pop out moves the reply to a compose window (for encryption, read receipts or complex formatting). Drafts are kept while Thunderbird runs; you are asked before quitting with unsent replies.

      

      

    


    
Signature

    

      
Use the signature you had in Outlook, exactly as it was, with its tables and pictures:
        
    
          
  • From a mail you sent from Outlook (best: works with new Outlook and Outlook on the web): in the message list, select one of your sent mails that has the signature, then click Use the signature in the selected mail.
    • 
          
  • From classic Outlook's file: on the Windows PC, copy the folder %APPDATA%\Microsoft\Signatures (each signature is a .htm file plus a _files folder with its pictures), then choose the .htm file.
    • 
        

        The pictures are stored inside the signature, so it works in the reading pane, the new-message pane and the compose window, and syncs to your other devices.

Mailbox cleanup

    

      
Free space in your mailbox: delete mail older than 3, 6 or 12 months, large mail, notifications and newsletters, mail unread for long, or the mail of one sender; choose folders, dates, sizes; see everything before it goes. Pinned and flagged mail is left alone. Also in the ⚡ menu and Tools → Clean up mailbox.

      

    


    
Reply, forward and pinned mail

    

      
      
      
      
      
      
      
      
      
      
      
Outlook Mode sets Thunderbird up once on each device: HTML compose (for @mention links), reply above the quote, and the Cards View (for the pin button). If you changed these back, this button sets them again.

      

        
        
      

    


    
Sending

    

      
      
A blue bar with Undo and Send now appears in the compose window. Pressing Send again also sends at once. Ctrl+Shift+Enter (Send Later) from the message body skips the countdown.

    


    
Scheduled mail

    

      
Schedule from the Send later button in the compose window. Scheduled messages wait in Drafts. Thunderbird must be running at the send time; overdue messages are sent at the next start.

@Mentions

    

      
      
Type @name in the message body to mention a contact (they are added to To). Mail where someone mentions you, from Outlook or from this add-on, gets the blue “@ Mentioned” tag. Show only those with Alt+Shift+M or the Quick Steps button.

      

        
        
      

    


    
Quick Steps

    

      
Run from the ⚡ Quick Steps toolbar button, right-click on messages, or Ctrl+Shift+1…5 for the first five steps. Order of actions: mark read → pin → reply/forward → move/archive/delete.

Keyboard shortcuts

    

      

        
ActionOutlookThunderbird

        
      

      
Thunderbird's own shortcuts always take priority over add-on shortcuts, so Outlook keys that Thunderbird already uses (Ctrl+F, Ctrl+Q, Ctrl+U, Ctrl+E, Ctrl+Shift+M) cannot be taken over; the closest free key is used instead.
